## Title (max 30 chars)
MyFinance - Budget Tracker

## Short description (max 80 chars)
Take control with a simple budget tracker for households: spending, debt & more.

## Full description (max 4000 chars)
MyFinance is a straightforward budget tracker built for New Zealand households who want a clear, honest picture of where their money goes -- without handing your bank login to a third-party app.

HOW IT WORKS
- Download a transaction CSV from your bank's internet banking (Westpac, ANZ, BNZ, and ASB formats supported)
- Import it into MyFinance and let your own keyword rules automatically categorise each transaction
- Review and adjust categories as needed -- your rules get smarter every time you correct one
- See exactly where your money goes, month by month, with no ongoing manual entry

FEATURES
- Import transactions from your bank's CSV export, or add them manually
- Automatic categorisation with custom keyword rules you fully control
- Budgets vs actual spending, with fixed and variable expense breakdowns
- Interactive spending breakdown by category, with drill-down into individual transactions
- Profit & loss view by month, year, or year-to-date
- Track mortgages, debts, and payoff goals over time, with a full balance history
- Track investments (KiwiSaver, shares, property) and net worth over time
- "Other Income" streams for a rental property, side business, or anything beyond your personal finances -- each with its own categories and budgets
- Share a household with a partner, with real-time sync across devices
- Light and dark theme
- Works offline, and syncs automatically once you're back online

WHY A CSV-BASED BUDGET TRACKER
Most budgeting apps ask you to link your bank account directly, handing your login credentials to a third party that then sits between you and your bank indefinitely. MyFinance never asks for your bank login. A CSV import takes a couple of minutes each week and keeps your data entirely under your own control -- nothing is shared until you choose to sync it to your own household account.

BUILT FOR HOUSEHOLDS
Most personal finance trackers are built for one person. MyFinance is built for two -- share one set of accounts, budgets, and categories with a partner, with changes syncing in real time across both of your devices. See the same numbers, agree on the same budget, and make decisions together instead of reconciling two separate spreadsheets.

BENEFITS
- Understand exactly where your money is going, every month, without guesswork
- Set realistic budgets based on your own past spending, not generic categories that don't match your life
- Track progress on debt payoff and savings goals over time, with a full history of every update
- See your full net worth picture in one place: bank accounts, investments, property, and debt
- Make confident financial decisions as a household, together, from the same shared numbers

PRIVACY & SECURITY
This app does not connect to your bank. You import transactions manually by downloading a CSV file from your bank's internet banking and uploading it here. Your data stays private to your household -- nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. Read the full privacy policy at getmyfinance.app/privacy.html.

PRICING
MyFinance is free to use for local, on-device budget tracking. An optional Pro plan adds unlimited history, cloud sync, and household sharing across devices, manageable any time from getmyfinance.app in your browser.
