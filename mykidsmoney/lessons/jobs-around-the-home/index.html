Jobs Around the Home

Some things we do because we're part of a family. Other jobs can be extra work someone chooses to pay for.

Two kinds of jobs

Responsibility vs paid work

Not every job around the home works the same way. Some things are just part of being in a family — everyone pitches in. Others are extra work that someone chooses to pay for. Both are valuable, just different.

One real family's example

🏠 Regular responsibilities

  • Dishwasher
  • Blinds
  • Recycling

Part of normal family life — not paid extra.

💵 Extra jobs that can earn money

  • Washing the car
  • Mowing lawns

Bigger, optional jobs someone can choose to pay for.

This is one family's approach — not a rule everyone follows. Families decide this differently, and there's no single correct system.

Why have jobs at all?

What jobs can help you practise

Taking on jobs — paid or not — can give you a chance to practise responsibility, reliability, following instructions, completing a task, taking pride in your work, and earning money. Doing a job doesn't automatically teach all of this on its own, but it's a real chance to build these skills.

For parents

If you're paying for extra jobs, it helps to set clear expectations up front:

  • Agree on the job
  • Agree on the payment
  • Agree on what "finished" means
