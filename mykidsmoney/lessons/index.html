How it works

The same idea, explained at every age

Lessons aren't one-size-fits-all. The same concept gets more sophisticated as a child gets older.

Question
"What is income?"
Young child
"Income is money that comes in. Your pocket money, or money you earn from a job, can be income."
Older child
"Income is money you receive, such as wages, business income, interest or investment returns."
Eventually
Concepts like gross income, tax and net income are introduced.

The lessons teach the concept. Where possible, the game will eventually let kids experience it — for example, a lesson on interest could be followed by "You have $50 saved. What happens when you earn interest?" That connection is part of the plan, not something built yet.
