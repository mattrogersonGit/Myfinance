Planning Ahead

Planning ahead means thinking about what you might need your money for before you spend it.

Things you can see coming

What's ahead?

🧸

🏫

Instead of spending everything today, you can plan for expenses you know are coming.

A real example

Spending it all today

$10You have $10.
$5You know you need $5 for a birthday present next week.

If you spend all $10 today, you won't have the $5 when you need it. What could you do instead?

One way of planning

A budget is planning ahead

A budget is one of the simplest ways to plan ahead — deciding where your money will go before you spend any of it.

⏱️ Short-term

Something you need soon — like a birthday present next week.

📅 Long-term

Something you may need or want much later — like a bike later in the year.

Try it yourself

Plan your $20

You have $20. A birthday present costs $5, a school activity costs $3, and you want to put $8 toward a savings goal. How could you plan your $20?

$5 + $3 + $8 = $16 planned, leaving $4 available for anything else. There's no single correct answer — the important part is thinking it through ahead of time.

For parents

Use upcoming real-life events as examples — an actual birthday, an actual school trip. The aim is helping your child understand that money often needs to be available at the right time, not just at some point.
