🪙 MONEY BASICS · LESSON 7

Where Does Money Come From?

Money doesn't just appear — it always comes from somewhere.

A child's world

Where did that $5 come from?

Say you receive $5 pocket money. Where did it actually come from? It came from money available in the household — your family chose to give some of it to you as pocket money.

If you earn $10 for washing the car, that's different: you did something useful, and someone paid you for it. That's earning.

Not quite the same

Building on what you know

It all connects to income

All of these — pocket money, earnings, gifts, sales — are examples of income: money coming in. If you haven't already, it's worth reading What Is Income? — this lesson builds on it.

A simple idea

Money moves around

When you buy something, your money doesn't disappear — it goes to someone else.

$5You have $5.
−$3You buy a $3 toy.
$3The shop receives that $3 — which they use to pay their workers and suppliers.
Doing something useful

How people earn money

People generally earn money by doing something someone else is willing to pay for:

Washing a car Mowing a lawn Working in a shop Building something Designing something Teaching Repairing something

Different jobs can pay different amounts — that's just about the work itself, not about how valuable a person is.

For older children

Grown-ups can have several sources of income at once: employment, self-employment, business ownership, interest, investments, and even rental income from property they own. No source is automatically "better" — they're just different ways money can come in.

Try it yourself

Think of three ways

Think of three different ways you could receive money.

Now ask yourself: which one would you have to work for? Which would be a gift? Which would be payment for something you sold?

For parents

You don't need to share exact household income to talk about this — everyday moments work well:

Receiving pocket money Paying for groceries Buying something Doing a paid job Selling something

The goal is simply to help your child understand that money has to come from somewhere.
