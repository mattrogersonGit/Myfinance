🤝 BORROWING & DEBT · LESSON 7

What Is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is a type of long-term loan commonly used to buy a home.

Why mortgages exist

Homes cost a lot

A house can cost a large amount of money. Many people don't have enough saved to buy a house outright, so a mortgage lets them borrow money and repay it over a long period instead.

The basic shape

How it fits together

House price
→
Deposit + mortgage
→
Home purchased
→
Mortgage repaid over time

Not every home purchase requires a mortgage — some people do save enough to buy outright — but it's a very common way homes are bought.

A simplified example

Deposit and mortgage

House price$500,000
Deposit$100,000
Mortgage borrowed$400,000

A deposit is money the buyer contributes towards the purchase up front.

Not free money

Interest and long repayments

Mortgages normally involve interest, so the borrower repays the money borrowed, plus interest, and potentially other costs. Mortgage repayments can continue for many years — which makes them a serious, long-term commitment connected to budgeting and planning ahead.

The trade-off

Owning sooner, owing longer

A mortgage can let someone own a home sooner than waiting to save the entire purchase price. In return, they take on a large, long-term debt and years of repayments.

Try it yourself

Work out the mortgage

House: $400,000. Deposit: $80,000. How much money was borrowed?

$400,000 − $80,000 = $320,000 borrowed.

Why would the borrower need to make repayments?

Because the $320,000 is borrowed money — it needs to be paid back over time, plus interest.

For parents

This is an introductory financial literacy lesson, not advice about buying property. It's a good chance to explain a concept children may hear adults discussing.

🎉

Borrowing & Debt complete!

  • Borrowing means using money now and repaying it later.
  • Debt is money you owe.
  • Repayments reduce debt.
  • Interest can increase the cost of borrowing.
  • Credit cards are a form of borrowing.
  • Loans are borrowing agreements.
  • Mortgages are long-term loans commonly used to buy homes.
