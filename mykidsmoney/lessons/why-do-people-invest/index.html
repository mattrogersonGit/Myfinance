🌱 GROWING MONEY · LESSON 2

Why Do People Invest?

People invest because they hope their money will grow over time or produce income.

Three simple reasons

Why invest at all?

📈 Growing wealth

An investment may increase in value over time.

💵 Producing income

Some investments may pay income, such as dividends or rent.

🎯 Long-term goals

People may invest for goals many years away, like retirement.

No guarantees

Investing can make money. Investing can also lose money.

Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Not a quick fix

Investing is often long-term

People often leave investments alone for many years, giving them time to potentially grow rather than checking on them constantly.

Why growth matters

Prices can rise over time

Prices for things people buy can rise over time — this is one reason people may want their own money to grow too, rather than simply staying the same amount. We'll cover this idea, called inflation, properly in a later lesson.

A simple example

Cash vs invested

$100Kept as cash — later, still $100, but some things may cost more.
$100Invested — could grow above $100, or fall below it.

This is the trade-off between staying the same and taking on some risk for potential growth.

Try it yourself

Why invest money you don't need right now?

Why might someone choose to invest money they don't need right now — future growth, income, long-term goals, or something else?

All of these are real reasons people invest. There's no single correct answer — different goals lead different people to invest for different reasons.

For parents

Avoid presenting investing as a way to get rich quickly. Emphasise patience and long-term thinking instead.
